I had the incredible opportunity to present two main track papers (including one spotlight poster!) and one workshop 
paper at NeurIPS 2025 in San Diego! The research I presented there spans areas of protein engineering and design, explainability, and generative AI. Below are some pictures of me with my wonderful labmates, as well as some beautiful San Diego views!
